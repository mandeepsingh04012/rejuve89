Our story

A neighbourhood juice bar for better days.

re.juve /89 started with a simple idea: Sector 89 deserved a spot where the healthy choice is also the tasty one. No powders posing as fruit and no syrups. Just fresh produce, a good blender and people who care what goes into your glass.

  • 🍉

    Fresh fruit, every morning

    Picked up daily and cut when you order, never the night before.

  • 👀

    Made in front of you

    An open counter. You see every fruit that goes in.

  • 🍯

    Your sugar, your call

    No sugar, less sugar or black salt masala. You tell us how you like it.

  • 💪

    Protein that tastes good

    A full whey scoop blended with real banana, mango and peanut butter.

    Good to know

    Questions? We've got answers.

    When do you open?

    Sunday, 1 Nov 2026 at 8 AM. After that we're open every day, 8 AM – 10 PM, at GF 02, Orris Market 89, beside Zudio.

    Do you add sugar?

    Only if you ask. Every juice can be 0 g added sugar: choose no sugar, less sugar or black salt masala. Prefer it sweet without sugar? Ask for honey (free) or dates (+₹10).

    How much protein is in a RE:Protein shake?

    Each shake has a full scoop of whey blended with real fruit or peanut butter. Tap any shake on the menu to see the protein for your glass size. An extra scoop adds about 24 g protein and 120 kcal.

    Can I order ahead?

    Yes. Order on this site or on WhatsApp, pick a time from 15 minutes to an hour, and collect at the counter. Pay by UPI, card or cash.

    How do I get there?

    We're at GF 02 on the ground floor of Orris Market 89, Sector 89, Gurugram, right beside Zudio. Get directions.

    How we make every glass

    0 g added sugar. Your call.

    No powders and no syrups. This is exactly how a glass is made at our counter.

    Sweet only if you ask, and how you like it

    • 🚫No sugarJust the fruit. Pick it on any juice or smoothie.
    • 🤏Less sugarA little, if you like it sweeter.
    • 🍯HoneyA natural sweetener instead of sugar. Free, just pick it.
    • 🌴DatesBlended in for a caramel sweetness. +₹10.
    • 🧂Black salt masalaThe tangy Indian way, on fresh juices.

    What happens to your glass

    1. Fruit is cut when you order. Never the night before.
    2. Made at an open counter. You see every fruit that goes in.
    3. Calories and protein are shown for your glass size (300, 450 or 600 ml), without added sugar. They're approximate. Tap a drink on the menu to see them.
    4. Protein shakes get a full scoop of whey, blended with real fruit or peanut butter.
